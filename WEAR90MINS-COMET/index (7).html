FAQ

Before you order

How do I place an order?

Add jerseys to your cart, then select “Order on WhatsApp”. Your cart is copied into a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Payment is agreed after that.

Can I choose long or short sleeves?

Yes. Every club jersey is available in short-sleeve and long-sleeve versions.

Which payment methods are available?

Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, and card payment can be arranged on WhatsApp. wear90mins does not collect card details on this website.

How long does delivery take?

Orders in Accra arrive within 1–2 business days. Delivery to other regions takes 2–5 business days.

Can I return a jersey?

Unused jerseys in their original condition can be returned within 14 days. Custom-printed jerseys may have different terms.
