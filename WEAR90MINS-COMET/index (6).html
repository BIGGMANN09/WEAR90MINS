Privacy policy

wear90mins respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the website uses and how orders are handled.

Information we use

The website saves your cart and saved items in your browser using local storage. This may include product names, quantities, prices, sizes, and sleeve options. It is stored only on your device and is not sent to wear90mins until you choose to order on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp orders

When you select “Order on WhatsApp”, the items in your cart are used to create a pre-filled message. WhatsApp handles that message according to its own privacy policy.

Payment information

wear90mins does not collect, process, or store card details on this website. Payment is arranged directly through WhatsApp after you place an order.
