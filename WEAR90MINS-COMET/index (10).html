Questions

Before you order

Can I order one jersey?

Yes. Single jerseys are available, but team orders of 10 or more receive priority production scheduling.

How long does production take?

Production takes 7–10 working days after you approve the design mock-up.

Can I add a name and number?

Yes. Every jersey includes a player name and number. You can also add a sponsor logo or team crest.

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Delivery is available across Accra. For other regions, delivery is arranged by courier and quoted at checkout.

What if the size does not fit?

Because each jersey is customised, we cannot accept returns for incorrect player details. Use the size guide before ordering.
